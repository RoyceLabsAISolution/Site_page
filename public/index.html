How it works

From first message to site visit, without the chasing.

  1. Buyer messages you

    On a WhatsApp Business number set up for your pilot, from your ad, board or listing. The assistant says it is automated and asks consent before saving anything.

  2. Requirement captured

    Budget, location, property type, size and timeline, understood in lakh and crore, cents and grounds.

  3. Matching listings shown

    Up to three listings from your own inventory. It only shows what you have; it never invents a plot or quotes a price.

  4. Your team takes over

    The buyer asks for a site visit. You confirm or decline in the broker console and call with the full picture.

What it always does

  • Says it is an automated assistant.
  • Asks consent before saving a name, number or requirement.
  • Treats silence or "maybe" as no.
  • Stops messaging the buyer on STOP or "no".
  • Hands over to a person for anything it should not decide.

What it never does

  • Quote or negotiate a price.
  • Take money, Aadhaar or other ID documents.
  • Book a visit without you confirming.
  • Message buyers who never contacted you.
  • Make up a listing it does not have.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does the bot replace my sales team?
No. It answers first, captures the requirement and hands a ready lead to a person. It never books a visit on its own, quotes a price, or takes money or ID documents.
Is it AI, and do buyers know?
Buyers are told up front that they are talking to an automated assistant, and are asked for consent before anything is saved. The WhatsApp assistant follows fixed, tested rules rather than free-form AI, so it will not invent a price or a listing.
Which languages does it speak?
English and Tamil replies. It reads Tanglish, and understands lakh and crore, cents and grounds, and terms like patta, chitta, DTCP and CMDA in buyer messages.
What happens to my buyers' data?
Nothing is stored unless the buyer says yes. STOP or no always revokes consent. You own your leads; we handle them only on your behalf.
Do you call my old leads?
Not yet. Outbound messages and calls stay off until the legal rules on consent and telecom have been checked by a lawyer. The pilot covers buyers who message you first. Re-engaging dormant leads when a new matching listing arrives is on our roadmap.
What about voice calls?
WhatsApp comes first. A Tamil and English phone agent is planned for a later phase and will follow once the WhatsApp pilot has proven itself.
Is this live with real customers?
We are opening the pilot now. The product is built and tested offline, and we will run the first live tests with pilot partners. We will tell you plainly what has and has not been proven.
