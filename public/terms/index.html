Terms of service

Last updated 14 November 2026. This is a plain-language summary written by Royce Labs. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and the written agreement we sign with each customer takes priority over it.

Who we are

Royce Labs AI Solutions, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Contact: hello@roycelabsai.in.

What the service is

We run a real-estate enquiry service for developers and agencies. When a buyer messages your WhatsApp Business number, an automated AI assistant replies in English or Tamil, asks for consent, records the buyer's requirement, shows matching listings that you provided, and passes site-visit requests to your team. Buyers are told they are talking to an automated assistant.

What it does not do

  • It does not quote prices, book visits on its own, or take money, bank details or ID documents.
  • It only uses the listings and approval details you give it. You are responsible for their accuracy.
  • It does not message people who have not contacted you first. Old-lead follow-up and phone calls are planned, not live, and will not start until the consent and telecom rules have been checked by a lawyer.

The pilot and fees

Applying is free. The first step is a small 30-day paid pilot in Coimbatore. We agree the fee with you by email, in writing, before anything starts. Nothing is charged until you agree. After the pilot you can stop, or move to a monthly plan plus a one-time setup fee, again agreed in writing first. Fees may change as we learn from the first customers, but never for a period you have already agreed.

The product is built and tested offline. Live use with real buyers has not yet been proven, and the pilot is its first live run. We will tell you plainly what has and has not worked.

Your WhatsApp number and your leads

  • The WhatsApp Business account and phone number are yours, registered to your business under your own Meta business account. We do not resell WhatsApp access.
  • The leads and buyer conversations belong to you. We handle them only on your behalf, keep each customer's data separate, and do not share one customer's chats or leads with another.
  • We do not use your buyers' data to train AI models.

A person is always reachable

The assistant is not a replacement for your team. Buyers can ask for a person at any time, and your team confirms every site visit. Our own contact for customers is hello@roycelabsai.in.

Consent and opting out

The assistant asks for consent before saving anything. A buyer can say STOP or no at any time and the assistant stops and treats consent as withdrawn. See our privacy notice and the DPDP consent guide.

What we need from you

  • Use the service only for lawful real-estate enquiries, and follow WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy.
  • Give accurate listings and approvals, and keep them up to date.
  • Do not use it to discriminate against buyers or to mislead them.

Stopping

You can cancel after the 30-day pilot with no lock-in, or earlier by emailing us. We can stop the service if WhatsApp or Meta restricts the account, if the rules above are broken, or if a law requires it.

No guarantees

We do not promise a number of leads or sales. WhatsApp, Meta and other providers can change their rules or have outages that we do not control.
