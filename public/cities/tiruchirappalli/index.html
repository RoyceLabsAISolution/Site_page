In short: Royce Labs is an AI assistant for real-estate developers and agencies in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. It answers property enquiries on WhatsApp: it replies in English or Tamil, asks consent before saving anything, captures budget, location and size, shows up to three matching listings from your own inventory and passes a site-visit request to your team. It never books a visit, quotes a price or takes money. Our first pilot is in Coimbatore; Tiruchirappalli is next on the list.

What Tiruchirappalli buyers typically ask on WhatsApp

Buyers usually write in a mix of English and Tamil. The assistant is built to handle questions like these, using only the listings and approval details you give it:

  • Is the layout DTCP-approved, and is there a RERA number?
  • Is there a plot near Srirangam, K.K. Nagar or Thillai Nagar in my budget?
  • What is the size in cents and in square feet?
  • Can the team show me the plot this weekend?

Areas and terms it understands

Common Tiruchirappalli areas buyers mention include Srirangam, Thillai Nagar, K.K. Nagar, Ariyamangalam, Thiruverumbur, Woraiyur. Sizes are usually in cents and square feet, and budgets in lakh and crore. The assistant also recognises approval and record terms in buyer messages, such as DTCP, TNRERA, patta and chitta. How it works for a Tiruchirappalli developer or agency

  1. Buyer messages you on a WhatsApp Business number set up for your pilot. The assistant says it is automated and asks consent before saving anything.
  2. Requirement captured: budget, location, property type, size and timeline.
  3. Matching listings shown: up to three from your own inventory. It never invents a plot or quotes a price.
  4. Your team takes over: the buyer asks for a site visit, and you confirm or decline in the broker console.
