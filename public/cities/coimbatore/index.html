In short: Royce Labs is an enquiry service for real-estate developers and agencies in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. It answers property enquiries on WhatsApp using an automated AI assistant: it replies in English or Tamil, asks consent before saving anything, captures budget, location and size, shows up to three matching listings from your own inventory and passes a site-visit request to your team. It never books a visit, quotes a price or takes money. The 30-day pilot is a small paid pilot, with the fee agreed by email before it starts.

What Coimbatore buyers typically ask on WhatsApp

Buyers usually write in a mix of English and Tamil. The assistant is built to handle questions like these, using only the listings and approval details you give it:

  • Is the layout DTCP-approved, and does it have a RERA number?
  • Is there a 3 cent or 5 cent plot in my budget, near Saravanampatti or Sulur?
  • Can I visit this Saturday or Sunday?
  • Is the plot corner or road-facing?

Areas and terms it understands

Common Coimbatore areas buyers mention include Saravanampatti, Vilankurichi, Singanallur, Peelamedu, Kovaipudur, Sulur, Thudiyalur, Ganapathy. Sizes are usually in cents and square feet, and budgets in lakh and crore. The assistant also recognises approval and record terms in buyer messages, such as DTCP, TNRERA, patta and chitta. It does not verify any of them; it shows only what you load. See the glossary of Tamil Nadu property terms.

It reads the area names and terms in a buyer's message; it only shows listings that you load. The area names above are examples of what buyers write, not a list of projects.

How it works for a Coimbatore developer or agency

  1. Buyer messages you on your own WhatsApp Business number, registered to your business. The assistant says it is automated and asks consent before saving anything.
  2. Requirement captured: budget, location, property type, size and timeline.
  3. Matching listings shown: up to three from your own inventory. It never invents a plot or quotes a price.
  4. Your team takes over: a buyer can ask for a person at any time, and you confirm or decline every site visit in the broker console.
