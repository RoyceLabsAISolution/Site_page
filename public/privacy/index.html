Privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026. This is a plain-language notice written by Royce Labs. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

Royce Labs AI Solutions, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Contact: hello@roycelabsai.in.

What we collect when you apply for a pilot place

  • Your name, business name, email address and city, and a mobile number only if you choose to give one.
  • Optionally: how many enquiries you get a week, and a short note.
  • The page you applied from, the language of the form, and the country your request came from (a coarse location from Cloudflare). We do not store your IP address.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about the pilot, by email (and by phone or WhatsApp only if you gave a number), and to reply to your application. We ask you to tick a box before we use your number.

Who sees it

The Royce Labs team. The application is sent by email using Resend, an email delivery provider, and the website is served by Cloudflare. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are in touch with you about the pilot, and up to 12 months after the last contact if you do not join. After that we delete it.

Your choices

Say STOP on any call or WhatsApp message, or email us, and we will stop contacting you. You can also ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will reply within 30 days.

Buyers who message our customers

This notice is about the website. Buyers who chat with a developer's WhatsApp assistant are told it is automated and asked for consent first; see our DPDP consent guide.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising cookies. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary cookies for security and performance.
