The real problem

Buyers message once. The first reply usually wins.

Enquiries land from ads, site boards and portals at all hours. Your team is on site visits, and the lead goes cold or goes to the next developer.

Replies that come hours late

A plot enquiry at 10 PM waits until tomorrow. By then the buyer has asked three other layouts.

Requirements lost in chats

Budget, area and size sit in scattered WhatsApp threads and notebooks. Nobody can say who asked for what.

Tamil and Tanglish get ignored

Most tools speak only English. Your buyers say "3 cent, 40 lakh" in whatever mix feels natural.

Consent & trust

Honest with your buyers, so they stay your buyers.

Real estate runs on trust. The assistant behaves the way you would want a new team member to.

What it always does

  • Says it is an automated assistant.
  • Asks consent before saving a name, number or requirement.
  • Treats silence or "maybe" as no.
  • Stops and forgets on STOP or "no".
  • Hands over to a person for anything it should not decide.

What it never does

  • Quote or negotiate a price.
  • Take money, Aadhaar or other ID documents.
  • Book a visit without you confirming.
  • Message buyers who never contacted you.
  • Make up a listing it does not have.

Outbound messages and calls to old leads stay off until a lawyer has checked the consent and telecom rules. We will say so plainly, rather than risk your number.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does the bot replace my sales team?
No. It answers first, captures the requirement and hands a ready lead to a person. It never books a visit on its own, quotes a price, or takes money or ID documents.
Is it AI, and do buyers know?
Buyers are told up front that they are talking to an automated assistant, and are asked for consent before anything is saved. Replies follow fixed, tested rules rather than free-form AI, so it will not invent a price or a listing.
Which languages does it speak?
English and Tamil, including Tanglish. It understands lakh and crore, cents and grounds, and terms like patta, chitta, DTCP and CMDA.
What happens to my buyers' data?
Nothing is stored unless the buyer says yes. STOP or no always revokes consent. Phone numbers are encrypted at rest. You own your leads; we act as processor under a written agreement.
Do you call my old leads?
Not yet. Outbound messages and calls stay off until the legal rules on consent and telecom have been checked by a lawyer. The pilot covers buyers who message you first. Re-engaging dormant leads when a new matching listing arrives is on our roadmap.
What about voice calls?
WhatsApp comes first. A Tamil and English phone agent is built for later and will follow once the WhatsApp pilot has proven itself.
Is this live with real customers?
We are opening the pilot now. The product is built and tested offline, and we will run the first live tests with pilot partners. We will tell you plainly what has and has not been proven.
